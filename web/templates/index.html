Multimodal Behavioral Analysis

A research prototype that measures how blink rate, gaze, head movement and voice change relative to a speaker's own baseline. It does not detect lies.

Key Features

😊

Facial Analysis

MediaPipe Face Mesh landmarks give blink rate (eye aspect ratio) and head-movement variance

👁️

Eye Tracking

Iris position relative to the eye corners gives a gaze-aversion ratio

🎙️

Audio Analysis

librosa measures pitch, jitter, shimmer, speech rate and pause ratio

🧠

Baseline fusion

Each signal becomes a z-score against the speaker's first 20 seconds; Gemini can optionally summarise the result

How It Works

1

Upload

Upload your video or audio file

2

Process

Signals are extracted in 5-second windows

3

Compare

Later windows are compared to the speaker's own baseline

4

Report

See per-signal deviations; uploads are deleted after analysis

Ready to Analyze?
